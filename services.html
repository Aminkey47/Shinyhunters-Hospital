Our Services

Quality healthcare services designed around the needs of our patients.

Healthcare Services

Shiny Hunters Hospital provides a wide range of healthcare services delivered by dedicated medical professionals in a safe and comfortable environment.

Emergency Care

Our emergency department provides prompt medical attention for patients with urgent illnesses, injuries and other medical emergencies.

Outpatient Services

We provide consultations, diagnosis, treatment and follow-up care for patients who do not require hospital admission.

Laboratory Services

Our laboratory provides diagnostic testing that helps healthcare professionals accurately identify and manage medical conditions.

Pharmacy

Our pharmacy provides prescribed medicines and professional guidance to help patients use their medication safely and effectively.

Maternity Care

We provide maternity services including antenatal care, delivery support and postnatal care for mothers and their newborns.

Surgical Services

Our surgical team provides professional surgical care, from pre-operative assessment through treatment and post-operative recovery.

Dental Care

Our dental services support healthy teeth and gums through examinations, preventive care and appropriate dental treatments.

Radiology and Imaging

Diagnostic imaging services assist our healthcare professionals in examining and monitoring different medical conditions.
